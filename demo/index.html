SignIR-Check Does the ASL gloss still state the facts of the English sentence? Pick a pair. The check runs live on this machine.

Two independent readings, one deterministic comparison

The English sentence and the gloss are each read into a fact record by a reader that never sees the other text. A fixed comparator then reports, fact by fact, whether the two records agree.

      Research prototype. It checks selected facts in written English and written gloss. It does not generate ASL, judge signing video, or certify grammar or naturalness. The examples are authored for demonstration and are not verified natural ASL. Accuracy on real translations has not been established yet; that needs the sealed held-out set and independent human labels.
